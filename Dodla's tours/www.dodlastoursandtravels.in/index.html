Est. 1962

64 Years of Sacred Service

In 1962, Sri Dodla Ramarao founded what would become Andhra Pradesh's most trusted pilgrimage company — driven by a simple belief that every devotee deserves a dignified, comfortable, and spiritually enriching journey to the divine.

Today, his son Sri Dodla Hemanth Kumar carries that torch forward with the same devotion and a modern touch — having escorted over 50,000 pilgrims across India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and beyond. Every yatra is led personally by him, maintaining the family promise of uncompromising care.

"We don't just plan tours — we create sacred memories. Every pilgrim we accompany is a member of our family, and we bring them home safely, spiritually fulfilled." — Sri Dodla Hemanth Kumar, Director & Tour Leader
